Tape vs Book — session 2026-09-10

The order book behind the day's runners (Databento MBO at the day-high). Book is T+1, so this is the latest readable session. 7/7 had a live book.
The tape's most important tell today was what *wasn't* there: across every runner the offers were REAL (cancel/add 0.8–1.0x), so nothing faded because a spoofer pulled a fake wall — the sellers were genuine and stood their ground. That distinction matters because candles alone would show TNON, AHMA, and PCLA rolling over and tempt you to call it distribution or a pulled-bid trap, when in fact price simply ran into real resting supply on the ask side (all three showed book lean -1% toward resistance) and got absorbed — TNON peaked +19% but held only +2%, AHMA gave back most of a +57% spike, PCLA the same. The clean runners tell the other half: DBGI had book lean flat-to-bid (+0%) with real offers and no overhead seller, and it ran +52% and held — the absence of a resting ask wall was the green light, not the size of the gap. SMCZ's two-sided/mixed book with neutral lean correctly warned of chop, and it went nowhere (-2%). The reusable pattern for tomorrow: when the offer is REAL and the book leans to the ask (resistance), treat spikes as fade/absorption candidates and sell into strength; when offers are real but the book leans bid/flat with no overhead wall (DBGI, and to a lesser degree PSIG/VIOT), the move is more likely to hold — and in every case, check whether a "wall" is actually being canceled (spoofed) before assuming it's supply, because none of today's were.
