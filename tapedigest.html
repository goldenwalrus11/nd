The reusable pattern for tomorrow: with cancel/add near 1.0x, treat the resting lean as truth — a runner with a seller camped on the ask (negative resting book) is a fade-into-strength short regardless of the opening pop, while a flat/positive book with no real overhead supply is the one that keeps going; ignore the peak print and trade the book's lean.
