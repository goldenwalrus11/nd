Tape vs Book — session 2026-08-17

The order book behind the day's runners (Databento MBO at the day-high). Book is T+1, so this is the latest readable session. 1/7 had a live book.
The order book only gave us a usable read on one name: TRUG, where the offer was genuine (cancel/add ~1.0x means resting sellers stayed put rather than pulling), and the book leaned -1% toward the ask — real resistance, not spoofed bait. That matters because the candles showed a +12% peak that then faded to -5% by 11:00, and the MBO tells you why: this wasn't distribution via pulled offers or fake walls getting lifted, it was buyers running into a seller who actually held the line and got filled into. In other words, the fade was real supply absorbing demand, not a spoof-and-drop — a very different setup than a wall that vanishes the moment price approaches it. The other six runners (IVF, WFF, AXTU/L/X, CDTG) had thin or absent MBO, so we're flying blind on them — the candles alone can't distinguish a held offer from a pulled one, which is exactly the edge we lack there. Reusable pattern for tomorrow: when a runner spikes into a resting offer with a low cancel/add ratio (~1.0x) and an ask-heavy book lean, treat the pop as fade-prone into real supply and don't chase the peak; when MBO is thin/absent, downgrade confidence and size down, because you can't tell a real wall from bait.
