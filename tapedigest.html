Tape vs Book — session 2026-08-31

The order book behind the day's runners (Databento MBO at the day-high). Book is T+1, so this is the latest readable session. 7/7 had a live book.
The order book's biggest tell was that none of these walls were spoofed—every runner showed a cancel/add ratio of 0.9–1.0x, meaning the resting offers were genuine sellers who held their price rather than bait that got pulled as buyers stepped up. That reframes the faders (AEHL, YDDL, NEOV) entirely: they didn't collapse because someone yanked support and distributed into a vacuum, they collapsed because they ran headfirst into real, persistent supply on the ask side—AEHL and YDDL both carried resistance-leaning books (-1% and -0%) and duly gave back their moves (-7% and -12% by 11:00 despite AEHL's +14% peak). Contrast that with NCRA and USDE, which showed bid/support-leaning books (+0%) and actually held or extended (NCRA +8% into a +47% peak), confirming the lean matched the outcome. The candles alone couldn't distinguish "buyer exhaustion into a pull" from "buyer absorption failing against a real seller"—the MBO showed it was the latter every time. The reusable pattern: when the book leans ask/resistance AND the offers are real (high add, low cancel), fade or stand aside because the pop is being sold into; only press longs when the resting lean sits on the bid with equally real size, and treat a peak print as a distribution level, not a breakout, whenever real supply is stacked above.
