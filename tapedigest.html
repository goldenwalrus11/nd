So the tell wasn't "is there a wall" — the walls were all real — it was whether the ask-side imbalance was defended (seller held → fade) or eaten (offers lifted → run). Reusable pattern for tomorrow: when cancel/add sits near 1.0x, treat overhead as genuine and stop looking for a spoof; then watch the resting bid/ask lean — a persistent -1% ask tilt that price stalls against means you're the exit liquidity and should fade the pop, while a -0% flat lean that keeps printing through offers means real demand is winning and you hold. Price alone showed you the +24% peak on NUWE; only the book told you that peak was someone selling into you, not breaking out.
