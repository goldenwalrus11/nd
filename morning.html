Shipping in the strait, a conduit for about one-fifth of global oil supplies before the war, has effectively collapsed, with between eight and 15 vessels crossing on early-August days versus roughly 130 transits before the conflict. The EIA does not expect Middle East oil production to return near pre-conflict levels until early 2027. This is a structural, multi-quarter tailwind for energy/shipping that the broad indices aren't pricing.

The AI-power-demand thesis keeps pulling real money into nuclear names — a slow-burn theme worth a watchlist.

The AI-energy convergence is becoming a distinct investable vertical, still under-covered relative to the pure-model headlines.
